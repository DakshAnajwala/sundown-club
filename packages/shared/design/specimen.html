Surfaces and ink

Four solid steps of one warm dark, tinted by the sky: amber-brown at golden hour, plum at dusk, violet-navy at night. Ink never changes, so its contrast is checked once per surface. Glass is the solid panel at 80% over the 3D, never a decoration.

Type

Young Serif names things and marks moments. Schibsted Grotesk does the work. IBM Plex Mono sets every number a player compares, and every key. Sizes are in rem, so they follow the reader's text-size setting.

Depth and shape

No hard borders. An edge is a faint inner ring with a brighter top, as if light catches the material; lift is a soft, offset, semi-transparent shadow that grows with the surface. The selected thing gets its game's colour as a ring, never as a glow.

Motion

Things enter with a strong ease-out, move with ease-in-out, and leave faster than they came. Interface motion stays under 300 ms; only a result or the sky takes longer. Only transform, opacity, blur and clip-path move.

TokenValueUsed for
  • Never animated: anything the keyboard repeats (game hotkeys, holding cards, the hub rail's arrows), numbers during play, focus rings, layout.
  • Reduced motion: travel distances become zero, scales become 1, and the long moments shorten to a 200 ms cross-fade. Colour and opacity still change, so state is never lost.
  • Springs: critically damped by default; a little bounce only after a flick or a throw.
